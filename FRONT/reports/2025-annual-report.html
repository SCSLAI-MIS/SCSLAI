In 2025, no new loan facility was introduced by the Association. Instead, SCSLAI implemented several enhancements and modifications to its existing loan products and credit policies to better address members’ financial needs and circumstances. These improvements were undertaken to provide members with greater access to credit facilities, increase the financial assistance available to them, and make the loan programs more responsive to their evolving needs. The modifications covered adjustments to loanable amounts, payment terms, and interest rates, which contributed to the increase in loan production during the year.

One of the significant enhancements was the increase in the maximum loanable amount of the Judiciary Development Fund (JDF) Allowance Loan from Php 100,000.00 to Php 130,000.00. This adjustment provided qualified members with greater borrowing capacity and additional financial support for expenses related to their allowances and other personal financial requirements. Similarly, the maximum loanable amount of the Enhanced Maxi Loan was increased from Php 500,000.00 to Php 550,000.00, enabling eligible members to access additional funds to meet their larger financial needs.

Furthermore, the Association revised its Credit Manual by reducing the required loan renewal percentage from 30% to 20%. This modification allowed eligible members to renew their existing loans at a lower repayment threshold, providing greater flexibility in managing their outstanding obligations while maintaining appropriate credit controls. The revised renewal requirement may also have encouraged increased loan renewal activity, thereby contributing to the growth in overall loan production.

Overall, while no new loan facility was introduced in 2025, the enhancements to existing loan products and the revision of credit policies contributed to increased lending activity. These initiatives reflect the Association’s continuing commitment to providing accessible and member-responsive financial services while balancing members’ borrowing needs with prudent lending practices and sound financial management.

The increase in dividends declared by SCSLAI from ₱235.48 million in 2024 to ₱257.39 million in 2025, or an increase of approximately ₱21.91 million, may be attributed to the enhancement of the Association’s loan products. The improvements in loan offerings, including increased loanable amounts and more favorable renewal terms, may have encouraged greater member participation and increased loan availments, thereby contributing to higher interest income. As the Association’s loan portfolio is a major source of revenue, the resulting improvement in earnings may have strengthened its capacity to declare higher dividends to members. Consequently, the dividend rate increased from 16.20% in 2024 to 17.00% in 2025, representing an increase of 0.80 percent. This reflects the potential positive contribution of enhanced loan products to the Association’s financial performance and its ability to provide better returns to its members, subject to the availability of distributable earnings and the applicable regulatory requirements.

Composite BSP Risk Ratings

SCSLAI remains committed to upholding the highest standards of good governance, regulatory compliance, and sound financial management, as well as maintaining the confidence and trust of its members and regulatory stakeholders. The Association recognizes the importance of BSP examinations as an essential mechanism for assessing the effectiveness of its governance structures, internal controls, risk management practices, and compliance with applicable regulations.

During the June 2025 BSP Examination, the Association achieved a composite rating of 3, reflecting the overall stability of SCSLAI in the areas of governance, operations, risk management, and regulatory compliance. This assessment serves as an affirmation of the Association’s continuing efforts to strengthen its policies, procedures, internal controls, and governance practices.

This accomplishment was made possible through the combined efforts of the current and previous Boards of Trustees, whose leadership and oversight provided direction in maintaining the Association’s policies and objectives. It was likewise supported by the commitment and collaboration of the officers, who ensured the effective implementation of the Board’s directives and regulatory requirements. Most importantly, the cooperation, dedication, and teamwork of all SCSLAI employees contributed significantly to the successful outcome of the examination.

The results of the examination also reinforce SCSLAI’s commitment to continuously improve its systems and processes. Moving forward, the Association will continue to address areas for improvement, strengthen its internal controls, enhance compliance mechanisms, and promote a culture of accountability and good governance. Through these continuing efforts, SCSLAI aims to preserve the confidence of its members and sustain a strong and positive standing in future BSP examinations.

Official SCSLAI Website

To better serve and assist our members, SCSLAI launched its official website as part of its continuing efforts to improve member service and strengthen communication through digital platforms. The website provides a convenient and accessible source of information about the Association, including details on available loan facilities, loan computation, and other services offered to members. Through the online loan computation feature, members can conveniently obtain an estimate of their loan proceeds and repayment obligations, helping them make informed decisions regarding their financial needs without the need for an initial visit to the SCSLAI office.

The website also serves as an official channel for disseminating recent news, announcements, notices, and other relevant updates concerning the Association. This enables members to stay informed of SCSLAI’s programs, activities, services, and important developments in a timely and convenient manner. The launch of the website marks another step in SCSLAI’s digitalization and modernization efforts, demonstrating the Association’s commitment to providing accessible, efficient, transparent, and member-focused services while responding to the evolving needs of its membership.

#SCSLAICares Community Outreach

On June 20, 2026, #SCSLAICares, in coordination with Pakay-Yakap, extended its support to the Dumagat Community through a community outreach program held at Sitio Manggahan, Daraitan, Tanay, Rizal. The initiative featured a feeding program, gift-giving, and fun-filled games for the children, providing not only essential assistance but also opportunities for joy, fellowship, and meaningful interaction. Through this initiative, #SCSLAICares reaffirmed its commitment to compassion, inclusive community engagement, and social responsibility by reaching out to underserved communities.

In July 2026, #SCSLAICares, in coordination with GenSan Youth Orchestra and Choir Corporation (GYOCC), extended its commitment to community service by providing relief assistance to communities affected by the earthquake in Mindanao. This initiative reflected the Association’s compassion and solidarity with those affected by natural disasters, recognizing the importance of extending timely support to communities facing difficult circumstances. Through this humanitarian effort, SCSLAI Cares demonstrated that its commitment goes beyond serving its members, embracing its broader social responsibility to help those in need and contribute to the recovery and well-being of affected communities.

On October 15, 2026, several SCSLAI employees participated in a blood donation drive facilitated through the Supreme Court Health and Wellness Center, as part of the “Dugo Mo, Buhay Ko” program of the Supreme Court Office of Administrative Services (OAS) and the Philippine General Hospital (PGH). Through their participation, the employees demonstrated compassion, volunteerism, and a shared commitment to saving lives. This initiative reflects the spirit of #SCSLAICares in promoting social responsibility and supporting meaningful efforts that contribute to the health and well-being of others.

For year 2025 anniversary celebration, the Association chose to extend its support to elderly individuals who are neglected, abandoned, or in need of care and support, housed at Kanlungan ni Maria in Antipolo City last November 7, 2026. As part of its anniversary activities and commitment to giving back to the community, SCSLAI, through its Trustees and employees, provided necessities such as food, toiletries, and selected medicines to the Lolos and Lolas residing in the home for the aged.

The facility is managed by dedicated religious sisters who oversee the daily needs, welfare, and well-being of the elderly residents. The visit provided an opportunity for the Trustees and employees of SCSLAI to personally share their time, resources, and compassion with the residents. More than simply providing material assistance, the activity reflected the Association’s commitment to its social responsibility and its desire to make its anniversary celebration meaningful by extending kindness and support to those who need it most. Through this initiative, SCSLAI continues to uphold the spirit of service, compassion, and community involvement embodied in its values.

The report includes key financial statements, such as the Statement of Financial Position, Statement of Comprehensive Income, and Statement of Cash Flows, along with relevant notes and disclosures. It provides insights into the Association’s financial performance, liquidity, and overall financial health.

The 6% or Php 17,426,752.05 increase in SCSLAI’s gross income in 2025 may be attributed primarily to the growth in loan production during the year. Although no new loan facility was introduced, the Association enhanced its existing loan products, particularly the JDF Allowance Loan and the Enhanced Maxi Loan, by increasing their respective maximum loanable amounts and modifying certain loan terms and interest rates. These enhancements, together with the reduction in the required loan renewal percentage from 30% to 20% under the revised Credit and Loan Review Manual, may have encouraged greater loan availment and renewal among eligible members.

The resulting increase in lending activity contributed to higher interest income from loans, which forms a significant component of the Association’s gross income. This performance highlights the role of the Association’s existing credit facilities and policy enhancements in supporting revenue growth while continuing to provide accessible financial assistance to its members. However, the sustainability of this growth remains dependent on prudent credit management, timely loan repayments, and the effective monitoring of credit risks to ensure that increased lending activity translates into sustained financial performance.
