2025 Treasurer's Report

Supreme Court Savings and Loan Association, Inc.

The SCSLAI 2025 Treasurer’s Report presents an overview of the Association’s financial activities and financial position for the year 2025. The report includes key financial statements, such as the Statement of Financial Position, Statement of Comprehensive Income, and Statement of Cash Flows, along with relevant notes and disclosures. It provides insights into the Association’s financial performance, liquidity, and overall financial health.

Gross Income

Particulars 2024 2025
TOTAL ₱269,104,819.19 ₱286,531,571.24

Increase

₱17,426,752.05

Percentage of Increase

6%

The 6% or Php 17,426,752.05 increase in SCSLAI’s gross income in 2025 may be attributed primarily to the growth in loan production during the year. Although no new loan facility was introduced, the Association enhanced its existing loan products, particularly the JDF Allowance Loan and the Enhanced Maxi Loan, by increasing their respective maximum loanable amounts and modifying certain loan terms and interest rates. These enhancements, together with the reduction in the required loan renewal percentage from 30% to 20% under the revised Credit and Loan Review Manual, may have encouraged greater loan availment and renewal among eligible members.

The resulting increase in lending activity contributed to higher interest income from loans, which forms a significant component of the Association’s gross income. This performance highlights the role of the Association’s existing credit facilities and policy enhancements in supporting revenue growth while continuing to provide accessible financial assistance to its members. However, the sustainability of this growth remains dependent on prudent credit management, timely loan repayments, and the effective monitoring of credit risks to ensure that increased lending activity translates into sustained financial performance.

Total Operating Expenses

Increase

Percentage of Increase

19%

The 19% or Php 6,931,583.44 increase in expenses from 2024 to 2025 may be attributed to several factors, including the allocation of retirement benefits for a former SCSLAI employee, salary increments granted to employees, and expenditures for promotional and marketing materials such as visibility in conventions and conferences. These expenses reflect the Association’s obligations to its personnel, adjustments in employee compensation, and initiatives to promote its loan products and services to members.

Collectively, these factors contributed to the increase in total expenses in 2025 and should be considered in evaluating the Association’s overall financial performance and expense management.

The increase in net income by 4% or Php 10,495,168.61 from 2024 to 2025 was primarily driven by the continued growth in gross income, largely attributable to increased loan production and enhancements to existing loan products. Although total expenses rose by 19% during the same period due to retirement benefit allocations, employee salary increments, and promotional and marketing expenditures, the increase in expenses had a relatively limited impact on the overall improvement in net income.

This indicates that the growth in the Association’s income was sufficient to offset the additional expenses incurred, reflecting improved financial performance and the Association’s capacity to sustain income growth while managing its operating costs.

Total Assets

₱279,292,284.70

Percentage of Increase

Total assets increased by 15%, or Php 279,292,284.70, from 2024 to 2025, primarily driven by increased loan production and the continued build-up of members’ capital contributions. The growth in loan production contributed to the expansion of the Association’s loan portfolio, while the sustained capital build-up strengthened its financial resources and capacity to support lending activities.

This increase reflects the continued utilization of SCSLAI’s resources to provide financial assistance to its members, alongside efforts to maintain a stable financial position. Moving forward, the Association must ensure that asset growth is supported by prudent lending practices, effective credit risk management, and adequate monitoring of loan collections to preserve asset quality and sustain long-term financial stability.

Dividend Rate

Particulars 2024 2025
Dividend Declared ₱235,484,967.55 ₱257,390,113.99
Dividend Rate 16.20% 17.00%

Increase in Dividend Rate

0.80%

The amount of dividends declared by SCSLAI increased from ₱235.48 million in 2024 to ₱257.39 million in 2025, representing an increase of ₱21.91 million. This increase may be attributed, in part, to improved financial performance resulting from enhanced loan products, including higher loanable amounts and revised loan renewal terms, which may have contributed to increased loan availments and interest income. As interest income from loans constitutes a significant source of the Association’s revenue, the resulting improvement in earnings may have strengthened its capacity to declare higher dividends.

Consistent with this increase, the dividend rate rose from 16.20% in 2024 to 17.00% in 2025, reflecting an increase of 0.80 percentage points. From an accounting perspective, the increase in dividends declared indicates a higher allocation of earnings to members, subject to the availability of distributable earnings, the Association’s financial position, and applicable BSP regulations and other relevant requirements.

As a financial institution regulated and supervised by the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP), SCSLAI remains committed to upholding applicable regulatory requirements, transparency, and accountability in the management of its finances. Our financial statements are also subject to independent audit by our BSP-accredited external auditor, Ms. Dolores Garcia, reinforcing the reliability and integrity of our financial reporting.

The Board of Trustees, officers, and employees remain steadfast in their commitment to prudent financial management, sustainable growth, and the continued protection of our members’ interests. For a more comprehensive view of the Association’s financial position and performance, members may refer to the 2025 Audited Financial Statements posted on the SCSLAI bulletin board.
