2023 Annual President's Report

Supreme Court Savings and Loan Association, Inc.

Financial Highlights

• LOAN PRODUCTION

To better serve and cater to members, especially those member-borrowers, we have lowered the interest rates of some loan facilities and reintroduced previously discontinued loan products.

Business Loan was introduced last February 2023 with an effective interest rate of 12% with a maximum loanable amount of five hundred thousand pesos (Php500,000.00) with a loan term of five (5) years.

The members of the Board of Trustees also reintroduced Housing Loan with an improved facility such as a minimum loanable amount of Php500,000.00 provided that this will be secured by the member's capital contribution (CAPCON). Housing loan is payable within five (5) years with an effective interest rate of ten percent (10%) per annum.

We also decided to lower the interest rates of some loan facilities to better suit the financial needs of our members. Petty loan was approved increasing the loanable amount to Php50,000.00 payable in one (1) year and with an interest rate of five percent (5%) per annum from its previous loanable amount of Php25,000.00 with 12% interest per annum.

Another loan with a lower interest rate is the Subsistence Loan. We decided to increase the loanable amount from fifty thousand pesos (Php50,000.00) to one hundred thousand pesos (Php100,000.00). The interest rate was reduced to 12% per annum instead of 16% and payable for two (2) years.

With these strategies, we increased our loan production from 9,675 approved loan applications in 2022 to 14,508 successfully processed loan applications in 2023, or roughly a fifty percent (50%) increase or Php236,556,138.60 in total gross amount.

• DIVIDEND RATE

SCSLAI continues to give back to its members by providing optimum returns on their investments. For 2023, SCSLAI was able to declare an annual dividend of 16.14% as approved by BSP last December 19, 2023, amounting to Php230,798,946.97. It can be noted that the projection made by the Board did not materialize due to limitations set by BSP in their recent examination. In this regard, they instructed that the management of the Association should ensure the following criteria before and after dividend distribution:

  • Sufficient funds are available to cover the payment thereof;
  • The Association shall not borrow funds to finance the dividend distributions; and
  • The distribution of the net income will not, in any way, curtail the Association's lending operations nor result in a liquidity problem.

These are the primary reasons why the Association only distributed an increase of 0.14% compared to last year even though the loan production was higher than 2022. This is done to ensure compliance with the BSP directive given in the Report of Examination which was conducted last April 2023.

Operational Highlights

• CONSTRUCTION OF THE SCSLAI EXTENSION OFFICE

Last January 12, 2023, we held the ribbon-cutting and blessing of the newly constructed office located at the back of the Main Building of the Supreme Court which was graced and led by Chief Justice Alexander G. Gesmundo. This was an indication that the Association was growing and improving to better accommodate the needs of the members. This serves also as the office of the key independent functions of the Association such as the Compliance Officer, Internal Auditor, and Risk Management Officer as well as the conference room for the members of the Board of Trustees.

This was funded by the Surplus Reserve allocated for Building Construction with a total contract price of Php2.7 million.

At this point, we would like to give emphasis to giving thanks to our very own Chief Justice Alexander G. Gesmundo, Atty. Maria Carina A. Matammu-Cunanan, Deputy Clerk of Court and Chief Administrative Officer, to our Clerk of Court En Banc, Atty. Marife Lomibao-Cuevas and Court Administrator Raul Villanueva for their invaluable support to SCSLAI's endeavor.

• MEMBERS' ENGAGEMENT THROUGH SOCIAL MEDIA PLATFORMS AND MOBILE NUMBER

In 2022, we have introduced the official Facebook page of SCSLAI to keep the members updated with the latest news and advisories. As of today, the page reached 1,300 likes and more than 2,000 followers. This was done to consistently utilize fast and effective information dissemination. Regular posts are made to this platform to keep our members up-to-date with the latest developments on SCSLAI's loan products, services, and other important advisories.

Thus, make sure to follow and like our Facebook account so you can be the first to be notified of important updates and advisories.

We also have an official mobile number aside from our PLDT landline for our members to communicate, especially for those who have concerns and have no internet access. On the screen, you may see the contact number that you can use to directly communicate with SCSLAI staff.

• BSP AUDIT RATINGS

Last April 2023, the audit team from the BSP arrived and examined the documents, processes as well as governance of the Association. Though some observations have come to their attention, as of June 2024, after a year of complying with all the requirements and committedly complying with their directives, we are now acceptable and partially acceptable in the four areas of their examination.

On that note, we would like to acknowledge the members of the Board, independent officers, and all of the SCSLAI staff for ensuring that we will be in adherence to BSP rules and regulations.

Looking Forward

Concluding 2023 with the success that we have achieved and the challenges we have triumphed, we look forward to 2024 with the fullest hearts and renewed vigor to deliver continuous development toward the achievement of SCSLAI goals and realization of its mission and vision.

2024 is the year of strengthening the Association's processes and revisiting the policies and procedures to better serve its members by having accessible and convenient ways of availing SCSLAI's loan products and services. As such, here are some of the initiatives that we can all look forward to for the coming year.

• DEVELOPMENT OF THE SYSTEM SOFTWARE

Since 2019, the members of the Board as well as the previous trustees already engaged with a third-party company for the development of the system software for our Association. After years of development, we are looking forward to its launch before the end of the year to better cater to the needs of the members by delivering a fast and more detailed loan application process. It can also help the Association in terms of generating reports and checking the financial reports needed. This development will also mark the Association's adaptability to technology and enhance its IT infrastructure.

• CORPORATE SOCIAL RESPONSIBILITY

As part of #SCSLAICares, we have also listed some projects for the community wherein we can contribute not only to our members but also to society as well.

Last November 8, 2024, the tree-planting activity was recently conducted at Brgy. Puray, Montalban, Rizal. Witnessing the effect of the latest typhoons on the province of Rizal, we have decided to coordinate with the Montalban Municipal Environment and Natural Resources Office (MENRO) for the tree planting activity. We believe that this simple act can support Rizal's campaign for reforestation and regreening and can be a small step to being involved in environmental activities.
